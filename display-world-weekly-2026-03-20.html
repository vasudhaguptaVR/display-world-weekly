MicroLED

CEA-Leti Develops Sub-Micron Perovskite Color Conversion Layer for MicroLED Microdisplays

MicroLED-InfoMar 18, 2026

Researchers at France's CEA-Leti institute have developed green and red-emitting thin-film perovskite color conversion layers (CCLs) using pulsed laser deposition, addressing a key limitation of quantum dots in MicroLED microdisplays — their low absorption and excessive thickness at very small pixel sizes.

  • Pulsed laser deposition (PLD) creates ultra-thin perovskite CCLs
  • Overcomes QD limitations: low absorption and thickness incompatible with sub-micron pixels
  • Enables green and red emission from blue MicroLED arrays
  • Critical for ultra-high-PPI microdisplays for AR headsets and wearables

  • Perceptual efficacy for fine-detail and productivity tasks still lags physical monitors
