Circuit Design

12-Bit 200 MS/s DAC with Novel Calibration Scheme for Microdisplay Drivers

MDPI ElectronicsMar 6, 2026

A new paper presents a 200 MS/s 12-bit current-steering DAC for high-resolution microdisplay drivers, introducing a Split–Sort–Symmetric Grouping Calibration (SSSGC) scheme to reduce current-source mismatch and improve static linearity without complex compensation loops.

  • SSSGC scheme reduces high-weight current source mismatch — no complex loops needed
  • Group-domain Dynamic Element Matching (DEM) suppresses harmonic distortion
  • Achieved INL: 0.79 LSB · DNL: 0.42 LSB · SFDR: 74.9 dB at 4.05 MHz output
  • Applicable to LCoS, micro-OLED, and micro-LED display drivers

Novel 12-Bit DAC Architecture Improves Microdisplay Driver Linearity

MDPI ElectronicsMar 6, 2026

Researchers published a 200 MS/s 12-bit current-steering DAC designed for high-resolution microdisplay drivers. The Split–Sort–Symmetric Grouping Calibration (SSSGC) scheme addresses current-source mismatch — a key challenge in driving high-PPI micro-OLED and micro-LED panels — without requiring complex feedback loops.

  • Problem: current-source mismatch degrades linearity in high-resolution display drivers
  • Solution: SSSGC reduces mismatch in high-weight sources; DEM suppresses harmonics
  • Results: INL 0.79 LSB · DNL 0.42 LSB · SFDR 74.9 dB
  • Applicable to LCoS, micro-OLED, and micro-LED driver ICs
