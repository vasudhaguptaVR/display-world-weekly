Counterpoint Research issued a significant market warning this week: OLED smartphone panel shipment growth is expected to stall in 2026, breaking a multi-year upward trend. MicroLED / Commercial

Samsung Micro RGB TV Architecture Explained: Why Independent RGB LEDs Beat QLED and OLED for Brightness and Lifespan

Samsung / CEProApr 13, 2026

Samsung's 2026 Micro RGB lineup uses a fundamentally different display architecture from both QLED (which uses a blue LED backlight with quantum dot colour conversion) and OLED (which uses organic emitters). Each pixel in a Micro RGB display contains independently emitting red, green, and blue micro-LEDs — inorganic emitters that deliver higher peak brightness (up to 4,000 nits), wider colour gamut, and a lifespan measured in decades rather than years. The R95H adds Glare Free glass and the Micro RGB AI Engine Pro for enhanced processing.

  • Independent RGB micro-LED emitters per pixel — no colour conversion or backlight
  • Peak brightness up to 4,000 nits — significantly higher than OLED
  • Inorganic emitters: lifespan measured in decades, no burn-in risk
  • R95H: Glare Free glass + Micro RGB AI Engine Pro + Motion Xcelerator 165Hz
