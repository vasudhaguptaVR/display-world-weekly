Samsung 2026 Micro RGB TV: Full Lineup Pricing — R85H from $1,599, R95H from $3,199

Samsung has revealed complete pricing for its 2026 Micro RGB LED TV lineup — eight models across three series. The entry-level R85H Series starts at $1,599 for 55 inches and goes to $3,999 for 85 inches. The premium R95H Series starts at $3,199 for 65 inches and reaches $6,499 for 85 inches. The flagship 115-inch MR95F carries over from 2025 at $29,999. All models use independently emitting red, green, and blue LEDs — a fundamentally different architecture from QLED or OLED — delivering wider colour gamut, higher brightness, and longer lifespan.

  • R85H Series: 55", 65", 75", 85" — $1,599 to $3,999
  • R95H Series: 65", 75", 85" — $3,199 to $6,499 (with Glare Free glass)
  • All models: 4K, VRR, Micro RGB AI Engine, Motion Xcelerator 144–165Hz
  • R95H adds: Glare Free, AI Engine Pro, HDR Pro, Motion Xcelerator 165Hz

Counterpoint Research · Apr 2026

OLED Smartphone Panel Growth Stalls in 2026 — Memory Cost Inflation Squeezes Handset Budgets

Counterpoint Research issued a significant market warning this week: OLED smartphone panel shipment growth is expected to stall in 2026, breaking a multi-year upward trend. The culprit is memory cost inflation — rising DRAM and NAND prices are forcing smartphone OEMs to cut costs elsewhere, with display upgrades being deferred. However, OLED in monitors (+45%), tablets (+13%), and laptops (+33%) are all expected to grow strongly, with Apple identified as the key driver of OLED notebook expansion.

  • OLED smartphone panel growth stalls in 2026 — first flat year since 2020
  • Memory cost inflation forcing OEMs to defer display upgrades
  • OLED monitors: +45% | OLED tablets: +13% | OLED laptops: +33% in 2026
  • Apple identified as primary driver of OLED notebook growth in 2026
